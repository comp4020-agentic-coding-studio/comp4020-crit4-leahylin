# Crit 4 reflection

**What was the breakthrough that moved the work forward?**

The main breakthrough was focusing on sound as part of the interaction, rather than just adding sound to the visual design. I decided to focus on two main actions: clicking a star and connecting stars. Clicking a dim star makes it light up with a soft crystalline sound, while connecting stars creates a melody. This made the constellation feel more like a real instrument. I also reused the existing Web Audio system instead of creating a new one, which kept the sounds consistent. Testing also helped me find small problems, such as the hint disappearing when the mouse only hovered over the sky and the stars staying bright after clearing the constellation. Fixing these made the interaction feel much clearer.



**What did this work change about who I want to be as a software developer?**

This work made me realise that I want to be a developer who thinks about the user experience, not just the code. Small details can make a big difference to how people understand and use a product. I also learned that testing is not only about checking whether the code works. It helps me find problems that I may not notice when looking at the code itself. In the future, I want to build software that is simple to use, carefully designed, and reliable, while also thinking about how each interaction feels to the user.
